Our Purpose

Company
Mission

Understanding why we exist and what drives us is the foundation of everything we build together.

1
Our Mission
We build tools that empower teams to do their best work. Our products simplify complexity so our customers can focus on what matters most.
2
Our Vision
A world where every team has access to thoughtfully designed software that removes friction and unlocks creative potential.
3
Our Values
Ship with intention. Default to transparency. Treat feedback as a gift. Optimize for the team, not the individual.
4
How We Work
Small autonomous teams, weekly shipping cycles, async-first communication, and a bias toward action over perfection.

Organization

Team
Structure

We operate as four core teams, each with a clear mandate. Teams collaborate closely but own their domain end to end.

E
Engineering
Builds and maintains the product. Responsible for architecture, reliability, performance, and shipping code. Works in two-week sprints with daily standups.
P
Product
Defines what we build and why. Owns the roadmap, gathers customer insights, writes specs, and prioritizes work across the backlog.
D
Design
Shapes how the product looks and feels. Leads user research, creates wireframes and prototypes, and maintains the design system.
S
Sales
Brings our product to market. Manages customer relationships, runs demos, closes deals, and feeds customer needs back to Product.

Getting Started

First Week
Checklist

Follow these five steps during your first week. Your onboarding buddy will help you through each one.

1
Set Up Your Accounts
Activate your email, Slack, GitHub, Linear, and Figma accounts using the credentials sent to your inbox. Enable two-factor authentication on all platforms.
2
Meet Your Onboarding Buddy
Schedule a 30-minute intro call with your assigned buddy. They will walk you through team norms, answer questions, and be your go-to person for the first month.
3
Complete HR Orientation
Finish all required paperwork, review the employee handbook, and complete the benefits enrollment process through the HR portal.
4
Attend Team Standup
Join your team's daily standup to introduce yourself. No need to prepare anything -- just say hello and listen to how the team coordinates work.
5
Ship Your First Contribution
Pick a starter task labeled "good first issue" in Linear. The goal is to go through the full workflow -- branch, build, review, merge -- by end of week.

Tooling

Tools
We Use

Our core toolkit is intentionally small. Four platforms cover communication, project tracking, code, and design.

#
Slack
  • Primary communication -- async messages, team channels, and quick huddles
  • Key channels: #general, #engineering, #product, #design, #sales, #random
  • Use threads to keep conversations organized
▶
Linear
  • Project management and issue tracking for all teams
  • Create issues, track sprints, and manage the product roadmap
  • Every task gets a Linear ticket before work begins
●
GitHub
  • All source code, pull requests, and code reviews live here
  • Branch naming: feature/your-name/short-description
  • PRs require at least one approval before merging
◆
Figma
  • Design files, prototypes, and the component library
  • Engineers reference Figma for specs; comment directly on designs
  • Design team maintains a shared library of reusable components
