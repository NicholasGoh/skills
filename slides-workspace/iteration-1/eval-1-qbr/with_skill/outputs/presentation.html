Risk Assessment

Key Risks

Three primary risks require monitoring and mitigation action heading into Q2. Each has an owner and response plan in progress.

HIGH Supply Chain Delays
Ongoing global logistics disruptions are extending lead times for key hardware components. Current delays averaging 3-4 weeks beyond standard timelines, impacting delivery commitments to enterprise customers.
Operations Customer Success Mitigation in Progress
MEDIUM Competitor Pricing Pressure
Two major competitors reduced pricing by 15-20% in Q1, creating pressure on new deal negotiations. Win rates held steady but sales cycle lengthened as prospects benchmark more aggressively.
Sales Product Marketing Pricing Review
MEDIUM Talent Retention
Engineering attrition ticked up to 12% annualized in Q1, driven by competitive offers in the market. Key senior contributors are flight risks without retention action.
