Risk Assessment

Key Risks

1

Supply Chain Delays High

Ongoing disruptions in key supplier networks may delay hardware-dependent deliverables. Mitigation: diversifying supplier base and increasing buffer inventory by 20%.

2

Competitor Pricing Pressure Medium

Recent aggressive pricing from competitors risks margin compression and slower deal velocity. Mitigation: emphasizing differentiated value proposition and flexible pricing tiers.

3

Talent Retention Medium

Elevated attrition risk in engineering due to competitive market conditions. Mitigation: revised compensation bands, accelerated equity refresh, and expanded growth programs.
