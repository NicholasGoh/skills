Risk Assessment

Key Risks
& Mitigation

Three primary risks require active monitoring and mitigation strategies heading into Q2. Each has been assessed and assigned to an executive owner.

High Supply Chain Delays
Ongoing disruptions in our supply chain are creating lead-time uncertainty and risk to delivery commitments. We are diversifying suppliers and building buffer inventory to reduce exposure.
Operations Procurement
Medium Competitor Pricing Pressure
Aggressive pricing from competitors is compressing margins in certain segments. Our response focuses on value differentiation and strategic bundling rather than a race to the bottom.
Sales Product Strategy
Medium Talent Retention
Attrition in key engineering roles is above target. We are rolling out retention packages, career development programs, and improving internal mobility to stabilize the team.
