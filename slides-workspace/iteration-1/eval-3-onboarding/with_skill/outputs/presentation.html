Organization

Team
Structure

We are organized into four cross-functional teams, each with a clear domain and shared accountability for outcomes.

⌨
Engineering
  • Builds and maintains our core platform and infrastructure
  • Owns code quality, testing, deployments, and uptime
  • Works in two-week sprints with daily standups
⚙
Product
  • Defines roadmap, priorities, and success metrics
  • Conducts user research and competitive analysis
  • Bridges customer needs with engineering execution
✎
Design
  • Owns user experience, visual design, and design system
  • Runs usability testing and interaction prototyping
  • Collaborates closely with Product and Engineering
☆
Sales
  • Drives revenue through new business and account expansion
  • Manages customer relationships and renewal cycles
  • Provides frontline feedback to Product and Engineering

Getting Started

Your First
Week

Follow these five steps during your first week to get set up, meet the team, and start contributing.

1
Complete IT Setup
Activate your company email, set up two-factor authentication, and install required software. IT will provide your laptop and credentials on Day 1.
2
Join Your Team Channels
Join your team's Slack channels, the #general and #announcements channels, and any project-specific channels your manager recommends.
3
Meet Your Onboarding Buddy
You will be paired with an onboarding buddy who has been here at least six months. Schedule a 30-minute coffee chat in your first two days.
4
Attend Orientation Sessions
Attend the company overview session on Tuesday, the tools walkthrough on Wednesday, and your team-specific onboarding on Thursday.
5
Ship Your First Contribution
By Friday, aim to complete a small starter task assigned by your manager. This could be a bug fix, a design review, a document update, or a prospect outreach.

Culture

Communication
Norms

How we work together matters as much as what we build. These norms keep us aligned and respectful of each other's time.

1
Default to Public Channels
Share context in team channels instead of DMs so knowledge is accessible to everyone. Use threads to keep things tidy.
2
Write It Down
Decisions, meeting outcomes, and context should be documented. If it is not written down, it did not happen.
3
Respect Focus Time
Check calendar status before pinging someone. Mornings are generally reserved for deep work across all teams.
4
Ask Questions Early
No question is too small. It is always better to ask and clarify than to spend hours going down the wrong path.
