Your First Week

First Week Checklist

  • 1 Set up your accounts
    Activate your email, Slack, GitHub, Linear, and Figma invitations. Enable two-factor authentication on every service.
  • 2 Meet your onboarding buddy
    You have been paired with a teammate who will answer day-to-day questions and help you get oriented during the first two weeks.
  • 3 Complete HR and compliance training
    Finish the required modules in our learning platform: security awareness, code of conduct, and benefits enrollment.
  • 4 Clone repos and run the dev environment
    Follow the README in our monorepo to get a local environment running. Ask in #eng-help if you get stuck.
  • 5 Ship your first small PR
    Pick a "good first issue" from Linear, make the change, and open a pull request. Your buddy will review it.
